Group Project Checkpoint

This checkpoint checks your group’s progress and each member’s workload. A completed model or polished report is not required.

Each group member must submit their own version on Moodle under this assignment. Submit a PDF of no more than two pages, including your group number, project title, name, and student ID. Describe the group’s progress and contributions from your own perspective.

Group progress: Briefly state your project question, dataset, work completed, and preliminary findings. Include one concrete piece of evidence, such as a plot, result table, or notebook/repository link. Unsuccessful results are also useful if explained.

Individual contributions: Provide a table with one row per member:
Name | Specific work completed | Evidence/output | Approximate hours spent | Next task

Be specific about each person’s contribution. Briefly identify any AI assistance and how its outputs were checked.

Next steps and blockers: List the remaining major tasks, who will handle each task, and target completion dates. Identify any difficulties or support needed.
